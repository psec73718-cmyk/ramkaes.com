About our school

Preparing every child for a bright future.

Richard & Marilyn Kindergarten & Elementary School is a nurturing institution founded on the belief that children learn best when they feel safe, respected and inspired. We combine strong early years practice with a welcoming community spirit.

Our curriculum is designed to support academic confidence, creativity, social development and responsible citizenship. Through guided learning, well-planned activities and caring teachers, every learner is encouraged to thrive.

  • Play-based and inquiry-driven lessons
  • Experienced, compassionate educators
  • Safe and inclusive learning spaces

Academic excellence

Core skills, confident learners and structured progress.

Creative learning

Arts, music and imagination are part of everyday growth.

Wellbeing first

Safe routines and caring support for each child.

Community spirit

Partnerships that strengthen families and school life.
